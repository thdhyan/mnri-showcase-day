Day at a Glance

November 6, 2026 — timeline overview of the showcase day.

09:00 – 09:10

University Leadership Welcome

ME 1130

Opening welcome from University leadership. Coffee + light snacks ready from 8:45 AM.

09:10 – 09:20

Director Welcome — Nikos Papanikolopoulos

ME 1130

Officially open the event, welcome attendees, set the day's purpose.

09:20 – 10:20

Faculty Lightning Talks I

ME 1130

Six 8-minute faculty talks across CS&E, AEM, and Bioproducts Engineering.

10:30 – 11:30

Faculty Lightning Talks II

ME 1130

Six more 8-minute talks — ME, CS&E, and Neurosurgery.

11:30 – 13:00

Demo Rotation — 7 Stations

Around Shepherd Labs

Self-select into 7 groups; drop into 12-minute demos at each station — drones, humanoids, underwater robots, mocap, stroke robot & more.

13:00 – 14:00

Lunch

Independent • Invited guests: Shepherd Lab 234

Independent lunch for attendees; Panera box lunch for invited guests.

14:00 – 15:10

Student Poster Session

Shepherd Lab 164 + Hallways

28 posters per final Plan C — 15 in the Drone Lab (P1–P15), 6 on its hallway-side outer wall (O1–O6), 7 in the 2nd-floor hallway (F1–F7). Talk with students and see live demos.

15:15 – 17:30

Round Table Talks

ME 1130

Funding opportunities talks (3:15–4:15), break, then industry talks: Honeywell, Medtronic, Mayo Clinic, Mortenson, PAR.

17:35 – 19:00

Closing Remarks & Reception

Drone Lab (Shepherd Lab 164)

Closing remarks from MNRI leadership followed by appetizers & networking. No alcohol in the Drone Lab.
